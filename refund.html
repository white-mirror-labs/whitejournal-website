Refund & Returns

Effective September 17, 2026  ·  Last updated September 22, 2026  ·  العربية

One product, one price, one honest policy. This page governs money: cancellations, returns and refunds. It uses the same defined terms as the Terms & Conditions and the Privacy Policy, and forms part of the Terms.

01

What This Covers

The terms below mean what they mean in section 02 of the Terms. For this page, these are the ones that matter:

the Journal The printed 90-day journal, 499 EGP, delivery within Egypt included. It is the only thing we sell, and therefore the only thing this policy refunds.
the App The companion app. Free to use; there is nothing to buy in it.
the Trial The first 14 days of a new App account. Free, and covered by section 02.
the Serial The single-use code printed inside each Journal. Section 06 explains what redeeming it means for a return.
02

The Trial Costs Nothing

There is no refund for the Trial, because there is no charge. The Trial takes no card and no payment method. It does not convert into a paid plan, it does not renew, and nothing bills you when the 14 days end.

We do not sell subscriptions of any kind. If you ever receive a recurring charge claiming to be from White Mirror Labs, it is not ours — tell us and we will help you trace it.

When the Trial ends, everything you have written stays readable at no cost, permanently. Guided meditation keeps working. Writing new entries, Insights, Vision and Practices wait for the Serial.

03

Before It Ships: Cancel Any Time

The Journal ships in print runs, so there is usually a window between paying and dispatch. While your order has not yet shipped you can cancel it for any reason, or no reason, and receive a full refund of 499 EGP. Nothing is deducted.

Email hello@whitemirrorlabs.com with the email address you ordered with. If your parcel has already been handed to the courier when we read your message, we will tell you and section 05 takes over instead.

If a pre-ordered Journal has not been delivered within 30 days of your order, you may also cancel within 14 days of that delay, even if it has shipped in the meantime, and receive a full refund.

04

Damaged, Defective or Wrong

If the Journal arrives damaged, misprinted, missing pages, or is not what you ordered, we replace it or refund it in full — your choice — and we pay the return shipping.

Tell us within 14 days of delivery. Send your order email address and a photograph of the problem; a photo is usually enough, and we will not ask you to post a damaged book back before we act.

A Journal whose Serial is unreadable counts here. We will issue a working Serial without needing the book returned.

This sits alongside the statutory warranty Egyptian consumer law gives you against hidden defects. It does not replace it.

05

Changed Your Mind: 14 Days

You have 14 days from the day the Journal is delivered to return it for a refund, without giving a reason.

As the Consumer Protection Law allows, this right does not apply to a Journal that is no longer in its original condition because of how it was used after delivery. In practice that means the Journal must come back:

  • Unwritten in — a journal is a book you write in, and a written page cannot be resold
  • Complete, with the Serial page intact and the Serial unredeemed
  • Undamaged after delivery

Opening the packaging and reading the Journal is fine and never costs you the right to return it.

Email us first and we arrange collection from you at no cost anywhere in Egypt. The 499 EGP purchase price is refunded in full.

06

The Serial

The Serial inside the Journal permanently unlocks the App for one account. It can be redeemed once and cannot be un-redeemed.

A Journal whose Serial has been redeemed cannot be returned under section 05, because the digital half of what you bought has been delivered and cannot be taken back, so the Journal is no longer in its original condition. Sections 03 and 04 are unaffected: a redeemed Serial never blocks a refund for a Journal that is damaged, defective or wrong.

If you want to return the book but have already redeemed the Serial, write to us anyway. We would rather hear the reason than lose you over a rule.

07

If a Parcel Never Arrives

Risk stays with us until the Journal reaches you. If a parcel is lost, or is marked delivered and is not in your hands, tell us and we will trace it, and replace or refund it at our cost. There is no need to argue with the courier yourself.

If a parcel comes back to us as undeliverable, we will contact you to arrange redelivery at no extra cost. If redelivery is not possible, we refund the full purchase price.

08

How to Ask

One email does all of it. Write to hello@whitemirrorlabs.com and include:

  • The email address you used at checkout
  • What you want — cancel, replace or refund
  • For a damaged or wrong item, a photograph

We acknowledge within 5 business days and tell you the outcome, with a reason if the answer is no. You do not need an account, an order number or a form.

09

How Refunds Are Paid

Method Always back to the original payment method, through XPay. We cannot redirect a refund to a different card or account.
Currency Egyptian pounds, the amount you were charged. We do not cover exchange-rate movement on a foreign card.
When we send it Within 7 days — of your cancellation for an order not yet shipped, or of the Journal reaching us for a return.
When it lands Typically 5 to 10 business days after we send it, depending on your bank. That part is outside our control.
Fees None. We deduct no restocking, processing or shipping fee from any refund.
10

What This Policy Does Not Cover

  • A Journal bought from anyone other than us — go to the seller you bought it from

  • Damage caused after delivery
  • Dissatisfaction with the outcome of the practice itself, as distinct from a fault in the book
  • The App, which is free and has nothing to refund
11

Your Rights Under Egyptian Law

This policy is written to sit on top of the Egyptian Consumer Protection Law and its executive regulations, never underneath them. Nothing here removes or reduces a right that law gives you — including your rights on a distance sale, and your right to a remedy for a defective product. Where this policy and the law differ, the law wins.

These Terms are governed by Egyptian law, and disputes are handled as set out in section 18 of the Terms. You may also complain to the Egyptian Consumer Protection Agency at any time.

This policy is published in Arabic and English. If the two differ, the Arabic version prevails.
