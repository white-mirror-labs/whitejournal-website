# Draft: Terms and Refund wording for the Digital Edition and pre-orders

Status: **DRAFT, not published.** Nothing on the live site changes until the owner (and counsel, if used) approves this and the wording is applied to `terms.html`, `terms-ar.html`, `refund.html` and `refund-ar.html`.

Why: audit item P0-3. The pre-order page sells a Digital Edition (249 EGP), but the Terms say "There is no separate digital edition" and the Refund Policy says the printed Journal "is the only thing we sell". The same pages also describe payment as an XPay card checkout, while the pre-order flow takes no payment online and confirms by phone.

## A. Questions the owner must answer first

The draft below assumes the answers in *italics*. Each one changes the wording; correct any that are wrong.

1. **What does a Digital Edition buyer receive?** *A single-use digital Serial, sent to their email after payment, that unlocks the App exactly like the printed Serial.*
2. **Does the Digital Edition unlock page scanning (OCR)?** Terms §04 says OCR needs the printed Journal. *Assumed: everything the printed Serial unlocks except page scanning.*
3. **How is a pre-order paid?** The page says "we call you to confirm your order and how to pay". *Assumed: we send an XPay payment link after the call, and cash on delivery is available for the printed Journal.* If cash on delivery is not offered, delete those sentences.
4. **When does the contract form for a pre-order?** *Assumed: when we confirm the order to you in writing (email or WhatsApp) after the call, as the current Terms already say for any order.*
5. **Digital Edition refunds.** *Assumed: full refund at any time before the digital Serial is redeemed; none after redemption unless the Serial does not work.* This mirrors how the current policy treats a redeemed printed Serial. Counsel should confirm it fits Consumer Protection Law 181/2018 and its executive regulations for digital content.
6. **Can a Digital Edition buyer later buy the printed Journal?** Not covered below; say if a discount or upgrade is intended.
