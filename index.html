What it is

A physical journal and a companion app, built as one practice.

The first product from The Mind. You write by hand, in a private space. The app helps you notice, organize and understand what you wrote.

Why it exists

Most of us feel more than we understand. A blank notebook gives room without direction. An app alone is one more screen. White Mirror Journal pairs the two, and leaves the meaning to you.

The Method

The M.I.R.R.O.R. Method.

The guiding framework behind the practice. From noticing how you feel to choosing what comes next.

  1. M
    Mood

    Notice how you feel.

  2. I
    Identify

    Name what is happening within you.

  3. R
    Reflect

    Explore what may be behind it.

  4. R
    Reframe

    Consider another perspective.

  5. O
    Observe

    Recognize patterns and changes over time.

  6. R
    Resolve

    Choose a meaningful next step.

The Foundation Edition

A guided journey,
for those who want structure.

Discover Yourself is a guided 90-day path in five phases. It is one way to use White Mirror. The journal itself stays open to however you want to write.

I

Days 1 – 18

Begin

You cannot change what you cannot see. Slow down and name the honest feeling.

II

Days 19 – 36

Look Deeper

Awareness without depth is just observation. Look beneath the first answer, to understand rather than blame.

III

Days 37 – 54

The Shift

You've seen clearly. Now you interpret differently. Rigid stories loosen into self-compassion.

IV

Days 55 – 72

See the Pattern

A recognized pattern changes a life. Triggers and loops become visible across time.

V

Days 73 – 90

Become

Self-awareness without action is autobiography. Choose one honest next step, and live it.

The Philosophy

A mirror,
not a judgment.

You don’t need technology to tell you who you are. You need space to understand yourself more clearly.

White Mirror exists to make that space possible.

We believe growth begins with awareness, change begins with understanding, and meaningful connection begins when people have room to be themselves.

The goal isn’t to become someone else. It’s to understand yourself well enough to choose who you become.

  • Feelings deserve to be understood, not judged.
  • Recognizing patterns creates opportunities for change.
  • Technology should support human agency. AI should help people reflect, not define who they are.

FAQ

Questions,
answered.

What is White Mirror Labs?

An AI-powered human development company. It builds products in three business lines: The Mind (self-awareness and personal development), The Body (physical well-being, coming soon) and The Community (human connection). White Mirror Journal is the first product, in The Mind. White Mirror Space, coming soon, belongs to The Community.

What is White Mirror Journal?

A physical journal paired with a companion app, built on the M.I.R.R.O.R. method. The journal is a private, flexible space to write and reflect without digital distractions. The app adds daily prompts, mood exploration, guided breathing and insights into recurring patterns.

Is the journal a 90-day program?

No. The physical journal is a flexible space you can use as you like. The Foundation Edition, Discover Yourself, adds a guided 90-day journey in five phases for those who want structure.

Does AI write or judge my entries?

No. Intelligent technology helps you reflect, organize your experiences and notice possible patterns. It does not tell you who you are, and it is not medical or therapeutic advice.

When can I get it?

Pre-orders are paused while we prepare the first run. Join the waitlist and you will hear first when White Mirror Journal is available in Egypt, with price and delivery details.

Can I use the app without the Journal?

Yes. The companion app works on its own, and works best alongside the journal. It opens soon; join the waitlist for early access.

Can I return it?

Yes. Once orders open, you will have 14 days from delivery to return the Journal without giving a reason, we collect it at no cost, and the refund is sent within 7 days. Damaged or wrong items are always put right at our cost.

Are my entries private?

Your entries are encrypted with a key of your own before they are stored. We do not sell your data, and we never use your writing for advertising or to train models.

Is the Journal in Arabic?

The first edition is printed in English, and its pages are designed to be written in whichever language your pen prefers. The Terms, Privacy and Refund pages are available in Arabic, and support answers in Arabic.
