The Method

The M.I.R.R.O.R. Framework.

Six dimensions of self-inquiry. Woven into every day of your 90.

M
Mindfulness

Present before the noise begins. Every session starts here.

I
Inquiry

The questions that make you stop. Those are the only ones that count.

R
Reflection

Looking back without flinching. Patterns only emerge when you look.

R
Responsibility

Own your choices. No exceptions, no audience, no excuses.

O
Openness

Honesty when honesty is the harder path. The practice only works if you do.

R
Renewal

End each day with intention. Not perfection. Direction.

The 90 Days

Five phases.
One transformation.

Ninety days isn't arbitrary. It's how long real change actually takes.

I

Days 1 – 18

Begin

You cannot change what you cannot see. Slow down, name the honest feeling, and notice what keeps returning. Day one is an arrival, not a transformation.

II

Days 19 – 36

Look Deeper

Awareness without depth is just observation. Look beneath the first answer, tracing feelings back to the old rules, needs, and wounds that shaped them — not to blame yourself, to understand.

III

Days 37 – 54

The Shift

You've seen clearly. Now you interpret differently. Rigid stories get reframed and insight becomes self-compassion. You stop rejecting parts of yourself and start relating to them with accuracy.

IV

Days 55 – 72

See the Pattern

A recognized pattern changes a life. You stop reacting and start watching. Triggers, loops, avoidance, repair — the patterns you couldn't see day to day become visible across time.

V

Days 73 – 90

Rise

Self-awareness without action is autobiography. The practice becomes the life. You choose one honest next step and live it. Day 90 isn't an ending — it's living from what you now know.
