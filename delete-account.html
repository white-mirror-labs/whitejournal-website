ACCOUNT

Delete Your Account

Applies to White Mirror — The Journal, on iOS and Android

You can delete your White Mirror account and everything in it at any time, whether or not you still have the app installed.

01

From inside the app

This is the fastest way, and it needs nothing from us.

  1. Open White Mirror and go to the You tab.
  2. Scroll to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

Deletion is immediate and removes your entries, your profile, your vision board pictures, and your social connections. It does not remove order records we're required to keep, and it does not un-redeem a journal serial — a serial is spent once and can't be reused on a new account.

02

Without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, or can't sign in, write to us and we'll delete your account on your behalf. Send the request from the email address your account uses, so we can confirm it's you.

Request deletion by email

We act on every request within 6 working days.

Email hello@whitemirrorlabs.com
03

What gets deleted

  • Your journal entries and reflections
  • Your profile (name, username, email)
  • Vision board photos
  • Meditation and practice history
  • Friend connections

Full detail on retention and what a small number of records we're legally required to keep looks like is in the Privacy Policy, section 14.
